Desk Planner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Desk Planner is a calendar and to-do app for Windows. This page explains what information the app uses and what happens to it. Back to the Desk Planner home page.

What the app can access

Everything works without Google. If you choose to connect your Google account, Desk Planner asks your permission for:

  • Google Calendar – to show your events, and to add, change or delete the events you create in the app.
  • Google Tasks – to show your to-dos, and to add, change or delete the to-dos you create in the app.
  • Google Drive (the hidden app-data folder only) – to keep your own computers in step with each other. The app cannot see any of your other Drive files.
  • Your Google account's email address, to show which account is connected.

How the information is used

Only to show it to you inside the app and to keep your calendar, to-dos and computers in sync. Nothing else.

Where it is stored

Your events, to-dos and settings are stored on your own computer, in the app's data folder. The sign-in that lets the app talk to Google is also kept on your own computer. Desk Planner has no server of its own: the developer does not receive, collect or store your data.

Sharing

Desk Planner does not sell, share, rent or give your data to anyone. It shows no advertising and has no analytics or tracking. Your Google data is not used to train any AI or machine-learning model.

The only other service the app contacts is the public NUSMods website (nusmods.com), and only if you choose to import a timetable. It is sent module codes only, never your Google data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Desk Planner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Press Disconnect in the app's Settings to stop the connection; the app also asks Google to cancel its permission.
  • You can remove Desk Planner's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete the data on your computer, uninstall the app and delete its data folder.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change. For questions, contact the developer using the support email address shown on the Google sign-in screen for Desk Planner.
