Desk Planner

A calendar and to-do widget for your Windows desktop.

Desk Planner is a small desktop app that keeps your week, your events and your to-dos in one window. It is a personal project, built for the developer's own use and shared with friends.

What it does

  • Shows your events and to-dos by day, week, month or as a list.
  • Optionally connects to your own Google account to show your Google Calendar events and to sync your to-dos with Google Tasks.
  • Keeps two of your own computers in step with each other, using a hidden app-data file in your own Google Drive.
  • Works without Google too: everything is stored on your own computer.

How it uses your Google data

Desk Planner only uses your Google data to show it to you inside the app and to keep it in sync. It does not send it to the developer or to anyone else. Read the full details in the Privacy Policy.
